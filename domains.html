ANET Names (.ant)

.ant names are an A Network Layer 1 naming system, not internet domains; browsers do not open them.

Register, manage and trade names on the A Network Layer 1. There is no registration fee, no commission and no network fee, and ownership is permanent. Registering, buying, bidding and receiving a name need at least 1,000 completed mining sessions and a sealed Layer 1 settlement.
