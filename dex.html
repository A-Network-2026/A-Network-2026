Cashout Guide (Wallet-Authorized)

  1. Complete 1,000 validated ANTS sessions to unlock cashout eligibility.

HOW THE BRIDGE WORKS
1
Lock on EVM
Send ETH / BNB / USDT to the A Network bridge contract on the source EVM chain. Your tokens are locked in a non-custodial vault.
2
Mint on ANET L1
The bridge relayer detects the lock event and mints the equivalent wrapped token (WBNB, WETH, WUSDT…) to your ANET wallet on the native L1 chain.
3
Swap on DEX
Use your wrapped tokens in the A Network native DEX pools — swap WBNB for ANET, provide liquidity, and earn fees.
4
Withdraw to EVM
Burn wrapped tokens on ANET L1 to unlock the originals on the EVM chain at any time.

FREQUENTLY ASKED
Do I need to convert ANTS to ANET first? ▾

No. ANTS are the base unit of ANET — 1 ANET = 100,000,000 ants. Your mined balance IS your ANET. No extra conversion step needed.

Where does my USDT go after the swap? ▾

It stays in your ANET L1 wallet. Verify on the explorer. Use Step 3 to move it to an EVM chain when Phase 2 launches.

Is there a minimum cashout amount? ▾

No minimum on the DEX swap itself. The bridge may impose a minimum to cover EVM gas fees — published when Phase 2 launches.

What is the swap fee? ▾

0.30% LP fee per swap — goes entirely to liquidity providers. No additional platform fee.
