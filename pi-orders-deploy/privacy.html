Privacy Policy

A Network Pi Order Book requests your Pi username and account identifier through Pi sign-in. The server verifies shared-order requests with Pi Network. The app does not persist your Pi access token.

Personal records stay in this browser and are removed when you clear site data. For shared orders, A Network stores the Pi account identifiers of the buyer and seller, deliverable, amount, due date, and delivery status on Cloudflare. Signed-in users with a valid invite link can see its terms while it is unclaimed. The first Pioneer to accept becomes the buyer. Share invites privately and avoid including sensitive information in deliverables.

Pi Network handles authentication, and Cloudflare delivers the site. Those services process requests needed to provide their services.
