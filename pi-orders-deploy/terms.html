A Network Pi Order Book is a tool for keeping your own records of Pi-priced purchases and sales. Shared orders record acceptance and delivery confirmation by the buyer. A Network does not transfer Pi, hold funds, verify payments, mediate disputes, or independently confirm delivery.

You are responsible for the information you enter and for checking payment and delivery independently. Closing the order book hides your records. Clearing browser site data removes personal records from this device. Shared orders are stored separately. Keep a separate copy of any information you need to retain.

The app is provided as available. Access and locally stored data may be interrupted or lost.
