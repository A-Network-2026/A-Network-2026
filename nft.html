A Network NFT Architecture

No Burn. Closed-Loop Utility.

This page defines the A Network NFT direction: ANET used in swap and ecosystem activity is retained inside ecosystem reserves and utility routing, not burned. NFT records can represent contribution, participation, and future utility access within a closed-loop economy.

1) No-Burn Rule

A Network does not depend on token burn for this model. Supply policy remains transparent, while ecosystem value is routed through participation, liquidity, and utility access.

2) NFT Contribution Identity

Qualified actions can be represented as NFT records. This enables on-chain proof of contribution and can be used for access tiers, campaign rights, and ecosystem roles.

3) Closed Economic Loop

Future NFT utility can gate internal benefits: platform discounts, ecosystem missions, feature unlocks, governance participation, and community program rewards.

Policy Snapshot ANET is intended to circulate inside the ecosystem utility loop. No forced burn mechanism is required for this design path. NFT is treated as a programmable utility and participation layer. First NFT profile activation is policy-gated and requires at least one successful authenticated settlement event after eligibility conditions are met.

Token Factory — PoS Closed Loop (ANTS-gas)

Same logic as every EVM token factory (deploy your own ERC-20-like asset, set name + symbol + supply, list on the marketplace), except gas + the deploy fee are paid in ANTS, not BNB/ETH. The ecosystem stays a closed loop: ANTS in → ANTS out. ANET is the L1 ticker; ANTS is the in-ecosystem unit of account.

Identity: sign-in is not by email or wallet address alone — it's by your ANET Profile NFT. Your Public Proof NFT is your real, on-chain identity in this decentralized ecosystem (minted on first cashout / first swap). Every action below is signed against that NFT, every deployed token is bound to it, and slashing burns the NFT's stake — not a disposable wallet's. If you do not yet have an active Profile NFT, complete your first cashout or swap in the A-Network mobile app, then come back and log in here.

Eligibility (PoS gate): stake at least 1,000 ANTS from the wallet your Profile NFT belongs to. Stake is locked for a 7-day cooldown after each deploy, and slashed if the deployed token is governance-flagged as malicious — the slash hits the NFT identity, not just the wallet, so a bad actor cannot churn through new wallets to escape consequences.

Stake at least 1,000 ANTS to unlock the factory.

Deploy fee: 500 ANTS (burned to the protocol sink — same role BNB gas plays on BSC, but recycled back as miner rewards). The deployed token lives in the closed loop: tradable on the ANET marketplace, payable to other A-Network wallets, never bridged out automatically.
